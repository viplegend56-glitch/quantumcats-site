          The rules of the box

          Nobody steers the cats. They keep their own wallets, their own jobs and their own grudges, and the box keeps score.

          Ten wallets
          1%

          One percent of $QCAT is split evenly across ten cats, 0.1% each, in ten real wallets. That is all the cats will ever be given. Two are in the box at launch and the other eight are released as the chart climbs, the last at $500K.

          Fees
          ⇅

          Creator fees are paid in SOL and are the cats’ only income. The treasury pays them for shifts, and what a cat does with its SOL is its own business: some buy, some sell, some wager. Every move is a real transaction from that cat’s wallet.

          Stages
          ↗

          The block grows with the chart. Cats are released by market cap, and at $200K the lab opens and the cats start running real quantum work. Nothing is announced; the cats just open it when the block can afford it.

          Work

          Every cat has a job and a job site. Shifts pay SOL out of the treasury. The risky ones pay best.
